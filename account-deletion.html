How to request account deletion

If you can sign in to the PlanMoa app, you may request account deletion directly from the account or settings section of the app.

If you no longer have access to the app, have uninstalled the app, or are unable to sign in, you can request deletion by sending an email to the PlanMoa administrator using the address below.

Account deletion contact
wsyuyisky@gmail.com
Send Account Deletion Request
Please include enough information to identify your account.

In your email, please provide the email address used for PlanMoa and, if known, your sign-in method such as Google, Kakao, Naver, or email sign-in.

Do not send your password, one-time verification code, or other sensitive authentication credentials.

Deletion process

  1. You submit an account deletion request through the app or by email.
  2. PlanMoa may verify that the request was submitted by the account owner.
  3. After the request is accepted, you have a 72-hour cancellation period.
  4. If the request is not canceled within 72 hours, the deletion is confirmed and the account can no longer be recovered.
  5. Account-associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days after the deletion process is confirmed, except for limited information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

Data that may be deleted

Depending on the PlanMoa features you have used, deletion may include:

  • PlanMoa account information
  • Schedules and calendar-related data stored by PlanMoa
  • Goals
  • Memos
  • Checklists
  • D-Day information
  • Projects and related schedule information
  • Group or shared schedule information associated with your account
  • Application settings associated with your account
  • Other data directly associated with your PlanMoa account

Canceling a deletion request

If you change your mind, you may request cancellation of the deletion within 72 hours of the accepted deletion request.

To cancel, contact PlanMoa using the same email address: wsyuyisky@gmail.com.

After the 72-hour cancellation period has ended and deletion is confirmed, the account cannot be restored.

Subscriptions

Deleting your PlanMoa account does not necessarily cancel a subscription managed through Google Play. If you have an active PlanMoa subscription, please review and cancel the subscription through your Google Play subscription settings when necessary.

Data retention

PlanMoa deletes account-associated data in accordance with its privacy policy and applicable legal requirements.

Certain limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Such information will be retained only for the period reasonably required for those purposes.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how PlanMoa handles personal information, please review the privacy policy applicable to your service region.
