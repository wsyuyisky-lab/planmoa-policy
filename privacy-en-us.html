4. Google Calendar Data

The Service accesses Google Calendar data only when you expressly authorize the integration. Calendar data is used to view, create, modify, and synchronize calendar events and to provide related scheduling features.

Google Calendar data is not used for unrelated purposes. You may disconnect Google Calendar at any time through the applicable account or app settings.

5. AI Assistant and Google Gemini API

AI assistant features are available to Premium members. These features may include schedule analysis, goal analysis, schedule creation, schedule modification, memo creation, productivity suggestions, and conversational AI assistance.

When you use an AI feature, information necessary to process your request—such as your questions, schedule information, goal information, memo information, and, where applicable, Google Calendar information—may be transmitted to and processed by the Google Gemini API to generate the requested result.

The Operator does not separately store AI conversation data on its servers. However, conversation data may be stored locally on your mobile device during the same day to maintain conversational continuity.

To maintain service stability and prevent abusive or automated use, Premium members may use the AI assistant up to 30 times per day. Free users do not have access to the AI assistant or call-recording analysis features.

6. Audio Files and Call Recording Analysis

You may upload an audio file or call recording to use features such as conversation analysis, automatic memo creation, task extraction, and automatic creation of related schedules.

Uploaded audio files may be transmitted to the Google Gemini API for analysis, and the resulting analysis is returned to the app in text form.

The Operator does not separately store uploaded audio files. Audio files are processed for the requested analysis and are not retained by the Operator for other purposes.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have all rights, permissions, and consents required by applicable law before recording, uploading, or analyzing a conversation involving another person.

7. Location Information

The Service may use location information to provide location-based schedule recommendations, regional information, and location-based notifications. You may deny or revoke location access at any time through your device settings.

8. Paid Subscriptions and Free Trial

PlanMoa distinguishes between Free users and Premium users. New users may receive a seven-day free trial during which all Service features may be available without functional restrictions.

After the free trial, users may continue using the Service as Free users or subscribe to Premium. Premium subscriptions are currently offered at KRW 4,800 per month or KRW 48,000 per year and automatically renew unless canceled.

Subscription cancellation, termination, and refunds are handled in accordance with the applicable Google Play policies.

9. No Sale or General Disclosure of Personal Information

PlanMoa does not sell your personal information.

The Operator does not disclose or provide your personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing or commercial purposes.

However, information may be processed by service providers such as Google when necessary to provide features that you request, including authentication, data storage, analytics, calendar integration, and AI processing. Such processing is performed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and is not a sale of your personal information.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a lawful request from a government authority.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

The Operator generally deletes personal information when the purpose for collecting and using it has been fulfilled, subject to applicable legal requirements and the account-deletion process described below.

Account Deletion and 30-Day Grace Period

When you request account deletion, your account is immediately deactivated. To prevent accidental loss of data and allow recovery from an unintended deletion, account and Service data may be retained for up to 30 days after the deletion request.

If you request to restore your account within this 30-day period, your account may be reactivated and your existing data may be restored. After the 30-day period, the account and associated Service data will be permanently deleted.

Deletion of Selected Data

PlanMoa provides in-app features that allow you to select and delete certain data yourself.
